# FlockWorld

**Toward Population-Scale Multi-Agent Video World Models through Artificial Life Simulation**

Meryem Koksal, **Yunzhe Wang**, Volkan Ustun  
**Creative AI workshop at NeurIPS 2026**

![A shared flocking arena and the synchronized egocentric views jointly generated by FlockWorld](/imgs/projects/flockworld.png)

FlockWorld studies whether a video world model can generate multiple agents’ first-person views that remain consistent with a shared world. A controllable artificial-life simulation provides synchronized observations and actions, making cross-view consistency measurable.

## My contribution

I originated the multi-agent video world model direction, conceived the FlockWorld testbed, and mentored undergraduate first author Meryem Koksal. I designed diffusion-transformer world models and built the JAX simulator, streaming data pipeline, video VAE training infrastructure, and evaluation workflow.

FlockDiT combines action-conditioned flow matching with causal temporal attention and cross-agent attention. It jointly generates egocentric views of a shared flocking world, with evaluation focused on whether those views agree about motion and relative positions.

I am continuing this direction with a larger multi-agent diffusion-transformer world model.
